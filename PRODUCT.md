# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Direct React/Vite implementation, replacing the Lovable approach at the user's request. No database, authentication provider, paid integration, or production backend is selected. The approved first build is a website prototype with clearly labeled grant/CME previews.

## Users

Prospective researchers seeking scientific research grants; healthcare professionals interpreting evidence; patients and families seeking accessible information and better conversations about care.

## Product Purpose

A proposed nonprofit foundation supporting scientific research, research grants, and education with the goal of better medical outcomes and better patient care. The September 21 direction removes the previous specialty-specific positioning and leads with research and grants.

## Positioning

Lead with scientific inquiry, transparent grants, and intended patient benefit. Education supports the research mission. Disclose the Evologics relationship and any product-specific study involvement. Final funding eligibility and scope remain to be defined; broader public positioning is not a finalized grant policy. Do not imply that a scientific question is already an established product benefit or that intended outcomes have already been achieved.

## Operating Context

The foundation is in planning. Grant amounts, eligibility, dates, entity status, leadership appointments, approved clinical content, and funded projects have not been finalized. The user reports eye-care accreditation through American Amnion Association and proposes AAA as an education partner. Its authority for the planned physician-credit activities and the final education arrangement remain unverified.

## Capabilities and Constraints

First build: navigable public pages, resource/topic previews, a grant application preview, and a future learning-area preview. No real applications, patient information, accounts, uploads, donations, email submissions, certificates, or credit awards. Form examples are non-transmitting, use synthetic data, and do not persist visitor input.

## Brand Commitments

- User selected Open Corners, variation 02, as the provisional logo.
- Evologics green and Ubuntu typography are explicit requirements.
- American Amnion is a style reference only, especially restraint, typographic contrast, and framing.
- Keep the foundation identity appropriate across areas of medicine.
- The foundation name and logo are working/provisional decisions, not registered or legally cleared marks.

## Evidence on Hand

Parent operating brief, content plan, and competitor research; supplied 2024 Evologics logos; inspected Evologics America and American Amnion homepages; approved concept board; provisional SVG reconstruction using actual Ubuntu outlines. There are no approved outcome statistics, testimonials, grantee identities, or physician-CME claims to display.

## Product Principles

- Make the three audiences' next steps clear.
- Publish useful education with review and source context.
- Explain funding relationships and research scope.
- Represent program availability accurately.
- Make the prototype useful for review without collecting real submissions.

## Open Decisions

Final entity/name, research administration and funding arrangements, specific products/questions, grant terms, editorial reviewers, CME provider/credit/platform arrangements, licensed photography, launch-ready content, and deployment. Implementation details in DESIGN.md are proposed defaults within the approved brand direction.
